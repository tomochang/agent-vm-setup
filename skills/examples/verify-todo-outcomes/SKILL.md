# Verify todo outcomes against three sources

## Never settle an outcome from the todo text

A todo entry records what someone *believed* when they wrote it.
Before moving, closing or reclassifying any entry, settle its
outcome against the primary sources:

1. **The messaging inbox** — read the relevant chat and paginate it
   to the end. The newest messages and a global search are not
   evidence that something never happened.
2. **The calendar** — check the events around the entry's dates.
3. **The mail** — look for the confirmation, cancellation, or reply
   that documents the outcome.

If all three leave the outcome unresolved, the entry is *unverified*:
it stays open, marked as checked-without-resolution in its log
section. It is never closed, and never reported as missing.

## Conflicts are resolved before they are surfaced

When sources disagree, cross-check until the evidence settles the
question. Only the genuinely undecidable point goes to the human —
with the evidence and the single open question, never a bare
discrepancy for them to untangle.

## Cleanup runs on proposals, not sweeps

Periodic cleanup works as a *draft*: a scheduled pass lists stale or
possibly-finished entries with per-entry evidence, the human approves
the batch, and only then are entries moved — through the same gates
as every other change.
