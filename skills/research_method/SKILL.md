# Research From Practitioners and Evidence

## Search for the best practice first

- Make your first move on anything a search for the established best practice. Anything means research, a build, a business idea, a process or a purchase.

- Look for how people who succeed at the task actually do it and what the standard playbook is.

- Reason from scratch only after that search, and adapt what you found to the case at hand.

## Filter sources hard

- Trust only facts, practitioners and real users for best practices. Facts are measured or sold data, practitioners are people actually doing it, and real users are people actually using or paying.

- Ignore theorists and authoritative scholars entirely.

## Build the list before the design

- Build the empirical list of what is actually selling or succeeding first, with the evidence attached to each entry.

- Design only after the list exists. Never let the design run ahead of the list.

## Handle missing evidence honestly

- Collect the facts first and act only where evidence exists.

- Label every estimate as an estimate and state its basis.

- Mark evidence you cannot obtain as 情報なし and exclude it from decisions, instead of filling the gap with plausible inference.

## Check for an existing decision first （既決事項の確認）

- Before presenting anything to the operator as an open decision or asking him to decide, search the records repo SoT first — private/ topic files, `todo_log.md` and other decision records — for an existing decision on that exact topic.

- If a decision is on record, present it as settled context with its date, never as a fresh question.
